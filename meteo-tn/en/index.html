2. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how the application Meteo TN (package: com.itaicoder.meteotn.app), developed by IT AI Coder, handles user information. IT AI Coder is committed to respecting user privacy and processing any data in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (hereinafter "GDPR") and applicable national data protection legislation.


3. DATA COLLECTED

Meteo TN does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data from its users.

The application does not require the creation of an account, does not request the entry of name, surname, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information, and does not collect usage data, analytics, or crash reports.

The only type of data that the application may use is the approximate geographic location of the device, and only when the user explicitly requests it by activating the location detection feature to display weather forecasts for their area. This data is never recorded, stored on remote servers, or transmitted to third parties: it is used solely in a transient manner to send a request to the weather API and is immediately discarded once the operation is complete.

If the user does not use the automatic location detection feature, no geographic information is acquired.


4. LOCALLY STORED DATA

Meteo TN may save certain user preferences and downloaded meteorological data in a local database on the device (Room database), solely for the purpose of enabling the display of forecasts without an active Internet connection and improving application responsiveness. This data:

- Remains exclusively on the user's device.
- Is never transmitted to external servers or third parties.
- Does not contain any personally identifiable information.
- Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the app's data through the Android system settings.

There is no remote backend associated with the Meteo TN application.


5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND EXTERNAL APIs

To retrieve weather forecast data, Meteo TN uses the open data service provided by meteo.report, as part of the official meteorological data made available by the Autonomous Province of Trento (PAT). This data is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0).

When the application makes a request to the meteo.report APIs, the service's server technically receives the IP address of the user's device, as is the case with any Internet connection. IT AI Coder has no control over such information, which is handled by the service provider according to its own privacy policy. Users are encouraged to consult the privacy policies of meteo.report and the Autonomous Province of Trento for information on how they process data.

IT AI Coder does not use any analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting SDKs, or any other third-party service that involves the collection of user data.


6. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

The only purpose for which location data may be temporarily used is to provide the user with weather forecasts for their geographic area, when explicitly requested by the user.

There is no processing of personal data for marketing, profiling, advertising, sale to third parties, or any other commercial purpose.


7. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Pursuant to Article 6 of the GDPR, the applicable legal basis for the temporary processing of geographic location data is the user's consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), expressed through the explicit action of activating the geolocation feature within the application and confirmed by granting the corresponding permission at the Android operating system level.

The user may withdraw this consent at any time by denying or revoking location access permission through their device settings. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

For data stored locally (preferences and weather cache), the legal basis is the legitimate interest of the data controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in providing a functional user experience, as well as the performance of a service requested by the user, since such data does not contain personally identifiable information and remains exclusively on the device.


8. DATA RETENTION

Since Meteo TN does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no retention policy applicable to data held by IT AI Coder on its own servers.

Data saved locally on the device (user preferences and weather cache) is retained until the application is uninstalled or the data is manually deleted through Android settings. The user is free to delete it at any time.
