2. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how the DC Monitor application (package: com.itaicoder.dcmonitor.app), developed by IT AI Coder, handles user information. IT AI Coder is committed to protecting user privacy and to processing any data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and any other applicable data protection legislation.

3. DATA COLLECTED

DC Monitor does not collect, transmit, share or store any personal data on remote servers or third-party services. The application does not use any external APIs, analytics services, crash reporting systems or any other data collection tools.

The only information generated during the use of the application is strictly technical and operational in nature, necessary for the proper functioning of the app, and is saved exclusively on the user's local device via a local database (Room Database). None of this information ever leaves the user's device.

Specifically, the application makes use of the following device features:

Push notifications: DC Monitor may send local notifications to the user's device in order to signal relevant events related to the ongoing monitoring activity. These notifications are generated entirely on the device and do not involve any communication with external servers.

Background processes: The application may run background processes to ensure continuous and timely monitoring of its intended features. In this case as well, no data is sent outside the user's device.

4. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

The features described above are used exclusively for the following purposes:

- Providing the core monitoring service offered by DC Monitor.
- Sending local notifications to the user in response to events detected by the application.
- Ensuring the correct background operation of the application.

No personal data is processed for profiling, marketing, advertising or sharing with third parties.

5. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

In accordance with Article 6 of the GDPR, the legal basis for the local technical information processing operations described in this policy is the legitimate interest of the data controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and the user's consent expressed through acceptance of system permissions at the time of installation or first use of the application (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), to the extent that such permissions are required by the Android operating system.

Since no personal data in the strict sense is collected or processed, the GDPR provisions relating to the processing of personal data do not apply directly. However, IT AI Coder chooses to adopt the principles of the GDPR as a reference standard to ensure the highest level of privacy protection for its users.

6. DATA RETENTION

All technical data and settings generated by the application are stored exclusively on the user's local device, within the application's Room Database. Such data is automatically deleted upon uninstallation of the application or upon clearing the app data through the device settings. There is no remote backend, no server and no cloud service in which user-related data is stored.

7. SHARING DATA WITH THIRD PARTIES

DC Monitor does not share any data with third parties. No statistical analysis, advertising, crash reporting or any other external service is used that would involve the transmission of data from the user's device.

8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

No transfer of data to third countries or international organisations takes place, as no data ever leaves the user's device.
