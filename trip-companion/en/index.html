2. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

This Privacy Policy describes how the Trip Companion application (hereinafter "the App") manages user information. Trip Companion is an Android application designed to assist travelers in planning and exploring tourist destinations.

IT AI Coder is committed to protecting users' privacy in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (hereinafter "GDPR") and applicable national data protection legislation.


3. PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED

Trip Companion is designed with a privacy-first approach. The App does not collect, transmit, or share users' personal data with the data controller or with any third parties.

All data generated through the use of the App is saved exclusively on the user's local device via a local database (Room Database). No data is sent to remote servers managed by the data controller.

The App does not use analytics systems, does not collect statistical usage data, does not use crash reporting tools, and does not track user behavior in any manner.


4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS AND THEIR PURPOSES

To provide its features, Trip Companion requests access to certain device resources. The requested permissions, their purpose, and the applicable legal basis are described below.

4.1 File and External Storage Access
The App may request access to the device's external storage in order to save and read travel data, favorites, and user-generated settings. This data remains exclusively on the device and is not transmitted externally. The legal basis for this processing is the legitimate interest of the data controller pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, as well as the performance of a contract or the taking of pre-contractual steps pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR. This processing is necessary for the proper functioning of the App's core features.

4.2 Photos and Video Access
The App may request access to the device's photo gallery and camera to allow the user to associate images with their destinations or travel notes. Selected or captured images are saved locally on the device. No images are uploaded to remote servers. The legal basis is the user's consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, expressed through the explicit granting of the permission requested by the Android operating system.

4.3 Push Notifications
The App may send local push notifications to the user's device to provide reminders, updates on saved itineraries, or other travel-relevant information. These notifications are generated locally by the App and do not involve external notification servers or third parties. The legal basis is the user's consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, expressed through the acceptance of notifications in the device settings.

4.4 Background Processes
The App may run background processes to ensure the proper functioning of certain features, such as updating local data or sending scheduled notifications. These processes operate exclusively on the user's device and do not involve any external data transmissions. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the data controller pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.


5. THIRD-PARTY EXTERNAL SERVICES

To provide certain App features, Trip Companion connects to publicly available third-party web services. It is important to clarify that these connections occur directly between the user's device and the servers of the respective third parties. IT AI Coder does not act as an intermediary in the transmission of data and does not receive copies of the information exchanged.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of the third parties listed below to understand how those parties process the information they receive, including the device's IP address, which is technically transmitted with every network request made to external servers.

5.1 OpenStreetMap and Nominatim
Trip Companion uses the services of OpenStreetMap and Nominatim for map display and geocoding features, that is, the conversion of addresses and places into geographic coordinates and vice versa. These services are operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation.
Privacy Policy: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

5.2 Overpass API
Trip Companion uses the Overpass API to retrieve information about points of interest (POIs) from OpenStreetMap data in the geographic areas consulted by the user. The Overpass API is a public service operated by independent third parties.
Further information: https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API

5.3 Wikipedia REST API
Trip Companion uses the Wikipedia REST API to retrieve summaries and descriptions of encyclopedic articles in multiple languages, in order to provide cultural and descriptive information about destinations displayed in the App. This service is operated by the Wikimedia Foundation.
Privacy Policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

Please note that every connection to these services involves the technical transmission of the user's device IP address to the servers of the respective third parties, as is the case with any Internet communication. IT AI Coder has no control over such technical transmission and does not process those IP addresses.


6. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

The permissions and features described above are used exclusively for the following purposes:

- To provide the App's core features, including navigation, search for points of interest, and access to information about travel destinations.
- To allow the user to save, organize, and manage their travel notes and preferences locally on their device.
- To send locally generated notifications relevant to the use of the App.
- To ensure the proper technical functioning of the App through background processes.

The App does not process personal data for marketing, profiling, advertising, or behavioral analysis purposes.


7. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

In accordance with Article 6 of the GDPR, the legal bases for data processing are as follows:

Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR (Consent): for permissions relating to access to photos, videos, and push notifications, processing is based on the user's explicit consent, expressed through the granting of the permission requested by the Android operating system.

Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR (Performance of a contract): for permissions necessary for the delivery of the App's core features, processing is necessary in order to provide the service requested by the user.

Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR (Legitimate interest): for background processes and storage access, processing is justified by the legitimate interest of the data controller in ensuring the proper technical functioning of the App.

The user has the right to withdraw their consent at any time through the permission settings of their Android device, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.


8. DATA RETENTION

All data generated through the use of the App is stored exclusively on the user's local device within the App's local database (Room Database). IT AI Coder does not retain any user data on its own servers or cloud infrastructure.

Local data is retained until the user chooses to delete it manually, or until the App is uninstalled, at which point the data associated with the App is removed from the device in accordance with the Android operating system's functionality.


9. TRANSFER OF DATA TO THIRD COUNTRIES

IT AI Coder does not transfer users' personal data to third countries outside the European Union or the European Economic Area.

Connections to the third-party services described above (OpenStreetMap, Overpass API, Wikipedia) may involve the technical transmission of the device's IP address to servers located outside the European Union. IT AI Coder has no control over such technical transfers. Users are encouraged to consult the privacy policies of the respective third parties for information on international data transfers.


10. RIGHTS OF DATA SUBJECTS

In accordance with Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, users have the following rights regarding their personal data:

Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): the user has the right to obtain confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning them is being processed, and, if so, to access that data.

Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): the user has the right to obtain the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data concerning them.

Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): the user has the right to obtain the erasure of their personal data in the cases provided for under the GDPR. Since data is stored locally on the device, the user may exercise this right directly through the App's features or by uninstalling the App.

Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): the user has the right to obtain the restriction of processing of their personal data in the cases provided for under the GDPR.

Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): the user has the right to receive their personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): the user has the right to object to the processing of their personal data at any time.

Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): the user has the right to withdraw their consent to processing at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR): the user has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Users within the European Union may contact the supervisory authority of their country of residence. In Italy, the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, available at https://www.garanteprivacy.it.

To exercise their rights, users may contact IT AI Coder at: it.aicoder@gmail.com. Since the App does not collect personal data on any remote servers, rights pertaining to locally stored data may be exercised directly by the user through their device's own functionalities.
