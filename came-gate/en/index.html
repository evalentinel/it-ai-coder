2. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

This Privacy Policy describes how the CAME Gate application (hereinafter "the App") handles user information, in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (GDPR) and applicable data protection legislation.

The CAME Gate App is an Android application that enables the management and control of gates and barriers using CAME technology. The App uses the device's location for geofencing features, which allow the automation of actions based on the user's geographical proximity to configured points of interest.

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the CAME Gate App and does not cover third-party services, applications, or websites to which the App may link or refer.


3. FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE: NO COLLECTION OF PERSONAL DATA

IT AI Coder expressly declares that the CAME Gate App does not collect, transmit, store on remote servers, or share with third parties any personal data of the user.

All data generated or used by the App, including data relating to the geographical location of the device, is processed exclusively locally on the user's device using the Room database integrated into the App itself. No data is ever sent to external servers controlled by the developer.

The App does not use analytics services, does not integrate user behaviour tracking tools, and does not use crash reporting systems that involve the transmission of data to third parties.


4. DATA AND PERMISSIONS USED BY THE APP

Although the App does not collect personal data in the traditional sense, its correct operation requires access to certain device resources. Each of these is described in detail below.

4.1 Precise GPS location and approximate location

The App requests access to the device's precise location (GPS) and approximate location. These data are used exclusively for the following operational purposes:

- Determining the user's distance from the points of interest (gates, barriers) configured in the App.
- Displaying the user's position on the map within the App's interface.
- Allowing the user to set up and manage geofencing zones.

Location data is processed locally on the device and is never transmitted to the developer's remote servers. The legal basis for the processing of this data is the user's legitimate interest in the correct operation of the App features expressly requested by the user (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), as well as the user's express consent at the time of granting the system permissions requested by the Android device.

4.2 Background location (Geofencing)

The App requests permission to access the device's location even when the App is not in the foreground (in the background). This permission is necessary solely for the correct operation of the geofencing features, namely to automatically detect when the user enters or leaves a predefined geographic area and, consequently, to execute automated actions configured by the user (for example, automatically opening the gate upon arrival).

Background location access only occurs if the user has configured at least one geofencing zone in the App and has explicitly granted the relevant system permission. The user may revoke this permission at any time from the settings of their Android device, which will result in the disabling of geofencing features.

The legal basis for this processing is the user's explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), expressed through the granting of the permission requested by the Android operating system.

4.3 Push notifications

The App uses push notifications to inform the user of relevant events, such as the activation of a geofencing zone, the status of a gate, or other operational events configured by the user. Notifications are generated and managed locally on the device and do not involve the transmission of personal data to the developer's remote servers.

Granting permission for notifications is optional and may be revoked at any time from the device or App settings.

4.4 Background processes

The App runs background processes necessary to keep the geofencing service active and to continuously monitor events associated with configured zones. These processes are strictly limited to the operational features of the App and do not involve any collection or transmission of personal data.


5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

5.1 OpenStreetMap and Nominatim

The App uses OpenStreetMap (https://www.openstreetmap.org) for map display and the Nominatim service (https://nominatim.org) for geocoding, that is, the conversion of text addresses into geographic coordinates and vice versa.

When the user uses the map or address search features within the App, requests are made to the OpenStreetMap and Nominatim servers. Such requests may include the device's geographic coordinates or the search terms entered by the user, as well as standard technical information associated with the network request, such as the user's IP address.

IT AI Coder has no control over the manner in which OpenStreetMap and the OpenStreetMap Foundation process such data. Users are encouraged to consult the OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy, available at https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy, and the Nominatim usage policy, available at https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/, for detailed information on data processing by these services.

The use of OpenStreetMap and Nominatim is governed by their respective terms of use and privacy policies. IT AI Coder undertakes to use these services in accordance with their respective acceptable use policies.


6. LOCALLY STORED DATA

All data generated through the use of the App, including configuration data, geofencing zones set by the user, preferences, and event history, is stored exclusively on the user's device using the Room (SQLite) database integrated into the App.

This data is accessible only by the App itself and is never transmitted to remote servers controlled by the developer. Deleting the App from the device results in the permanent removal of all locally stored data.

The user has full control over their local data and may delete it at any time through the App's data management features or by uninstalling the App.


7. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

Location data and other technical data processed by the App are used exclusively for the following purposes:

- Providing the App's core features, including gate and barrier control.
- Operating the geofencing system for the automation of user actions.
- Displaying maps and supporting address searches via OpenStreetMap and Nominatim.
- Sending local notifications related to operational events configured by the user.

No profiling, marketing, behavioural analysis, or data selling purposes are pursued.


8. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

In accordance with Art. 6 GDPR, the legal bases for the processing of technical data by the App are as follows:

User's consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for access to background location required for geofencing and for the sending of push notifications. Consent is expressed by the user through the granting of system permissions requested by the Android device.

Performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b)): to the extent that processing is necessary to provide the App features requested by the user.

Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): for the local processing of technical data necessary for the correct operation of the App, to the extent that such processing does not prejudice the fundamental rights and freedoms of the user.


9. DATA RETENTION PERIOD

Data remains on the user's device until the user decides to delete it, through the App's features or by uninstalling the App.
